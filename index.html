📚 Learning Objectives & Curriculum

OrganizeOS introduces computer literacy, taxonomies, and folder hierarchies across three structured learning stages:

Tier 1: Young Explorers (K-5)

Focuses on object recognition and intuitive grouping (pictures vs. homework vs. songs) with large cards and forgiving feedback.

Tier 2: Digital Ranger (Grades 6-8)

Introduces file extensions (.docx, .pdf, .mp4), project versioning (Drafts vs. Final Submissions), and research categorization.

Tier 3: IT Architect (High School & Advanced)

Simulates real-world developer directories (`/src`, `/etc`, `/assets`, `/logs`), POSIX-style paths, and strict technical extensions.

🛠️ Teacher & Custom Level Editor

Quickly append a custom file to the desktop to test your students on specific subject units.
